Ask your notes. See the page it came from.

Cerebro reads the PDFs you study from, explains them in plain words, and quizzes you on them. Every answer points to the page that backs it up.

You'll add your PDF on the next screen.
  • Answers cite pages. Tap a page tab to open your PDF right there.
  • Extra explanations are labeled. Anything not in your PDF sits under "Beyond your PDF".
  • Quizzes are checked against the text. Questions whose answer the PDF doesn't support are thrown out.
  • Runs on your computer. Your documents stay with your local Ollama models.

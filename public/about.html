About Cerebro

A quieter way to work through a document.

Cerebro pairs local PDF search with a chat model you run through Ollama.

The workflow

From page to answer

  1. 01

    Add your PDFs

    Upload text-based course materials to the conversation you are working in.

  2. 02

    Choose your sources

    Focus on the files that matter now. The others stay available.

  3. 03

    Ask a question

    Cerebro retrieves passages and checks model-selected quotes against the source.
